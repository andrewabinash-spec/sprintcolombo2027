03 — Campaign goal

800–1,000

completed registrations by race day.

800 is our base target and 1,000 our stretch. Both count deduplicated entries from our paid and organic social media, creators, ambassadors and tracked community partners.

Cumulative checkpoints (base – stretch)

80–100End Oct
260–325End Nov
480–600End Dec
800–1,00031 Jan

There is no historical data on social-media-attributed registrations for Sprint Colombo, so these are planning targets, not a forecast or guarantee. With USD 100 a month in paid media, ads alone won't deliver them: most entries come from partners, creators and organic reach. We'll reassess after the first two to three weeks and rephase checkpoints once the registration date is confirmed.

17 — Open-water safety & beginner education

Accessible, never careless.

Open Water 101

A series made with qualified swimming and safety partners, covering realistic preparation, equipment, supervised practice and following official safety instructions.

  • Expert-led content only
  • Never suggest any distance is safe without the right ability and preparation
  • Final safety, course and eligibility details published only after Serendib confirms them
